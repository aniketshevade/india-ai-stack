# India's AI stack

An interactive 3D figure of India's AI compute stack: the IndiaAI Mission's GPU pool, the open market around it, and the money that flows between them.

**View it:** https://aniketshevade.github.io/india-ai-stack/

## What the money flows show, and what they don't

- Only flows with a published figure are drawn. Most private AI revenue in India, and most of what firms pay each other, is not disclosed, so the open market looks thinner than it is.
- Each flow card gives the headline figure, every figure behind it, its stage (budget, commitment, award ceiling, pledge, money moved, estimate), period, status and source, and a caveat on what the number covers.
- Budgets, commitments, award ceilings, pledges and money moved are never added together.
- Every flow was checked against its sources on 6 November 2026.

The layers are adapted from Vashishth Doshi's [Compute Governance Explainer](https://vashishthdoshi.github.io/compute-governance-explainer/), an independent explainer, not an official standard. Logos belong to their owners and appear only to identify each organisation.

Figures as of November 2026.
